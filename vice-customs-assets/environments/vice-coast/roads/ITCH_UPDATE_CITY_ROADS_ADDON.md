### Drawing reference PNG sheets added

This pack now includes extra drawing reference PNG sheets prepared by Eclair Assets.

The reference add-on includes:

- one top-view shaded sheet for all 72 road models
- one top-view silhouette sheet
- individual four-view PNG cards for each model
- top, front, 3/4, and side views

These PNG sheets are meant for artists who want to check road shapes quickly before opening the 3D files.
They can also help with manga panels, webtoon backgrounds, city layouts, traffic scenes, and quick perspective planning.

The PNG sheets were generated from the included CC0 GLB models by Eclair Assets. They are not copied preview images from the original source page.
